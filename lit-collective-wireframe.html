BRAND POSITIONING & GTM

In 2024, we led the repositioning of Lit Collective to sharpen our differentiation in a changing tech market. We narrowed the target audience, refined the niche, and aligned brand narrative, visual identity, and sales processes around a clearer value proposition.

Project
Snapshot
Year: 2024
Project type: Tech design studio · B2B
Team: Lit Collective in-house team (brand, positioning & GTM)
Channels: Website, social media (X and LinkedIn), sales decks, internal comms
Brand repositioning Value proposition Messaging system Content system GTM alignment
What we did
  • Ran competitive research to find the positioning white space
  • Rebuilt brand narrative, messaging, and positioning
  • Created a messaging system usable across sales, web, and content
  • Aligned sales and marketing assets with the refined positioning
Main challenges
  • Repositioning without disrupting active client delivery
  • Building a system the team could use with autonomy
  • Operating under bear market constraints and reduced budgets
  • Differentiating in a saturated market

LAUNCH CAMPAIGN

In 2023, we led the end-to-end launch campaign for Lit Academy — a free design bootcamp built as the top-of-funnel for a paid education platform, using education as the main acquisition channel. Result: 1,200+ registrations in 15 days and ~50% average live attendance across 10 sessions.

Project
Snapshot
Year: 2023
Project type: Education-led launch campaign
Format: Free online bootcamp (2 weeks, 10 live sessions)
Team: Lit Collective in-house team — 5 people (content & live production)
Channels: Instagram, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Email, Telegram, Twitter, StreamYard, Twitch
Launch strategy Acquisition strategy Program strategy Paid media Community building
What we did
  • Defined the launch strategy
  • Structured paid media, automation, and community workflows to drive registrations and retention
  • Led execution across content and live delivery
  • Managed a team of 5 across content production and Twitch live sessions
Main challenges
  • No existing audience, launching to a cold market
  • Keeping attendance high across 10 consecutive sessions
  • Coordinating live production with an in-house team
  • Balancing free value with paid platform conversion intent
